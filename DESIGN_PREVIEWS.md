# Local appearance previews

Open http://127.0.0.1:5173 and click the palette icon in the top toolbar. Choose a look to apply it throughout the interface, including the library, companion, notes, dialogs, and controls. The Fonts tab offers 15 typefaces with live sample cards. The Colors tab changes the palette without resetting your chosen font. Your selection is saved locally; PDF content retains its original formatting.

The previews share one implementation. Fonts are bundled as WOFF2 files and work offline; their licenses are included in `public/fonts/`. Included in the Linux desktop app starting with v0.3.0.

Available fonts: Inter, Manrope, IBM Plex Sans, DM Sans, Source Sans 3, Nunito Sans, Work Sans, Outfit, Roboto, Open Sans, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, Lora, Literata, Merriweather, and JetBrains Mono.
